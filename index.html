01 · Background

About Me

A progression from learning how code works to building systems that have to work together.

I started programming at 13. My first language was C# for Unity, which gave me an early introduction to building interactive software. Later, at 15, I learned C++ at the "Grigore Moisil" Theoretical High School of Informatics in Iași.

Since then, my interests have expanded toward competitive programming, algorithms, systems programming and full-stack development. I especially enjoy projects where the interesting part is not just writing code, but designing the pieces so that they work together reliably.
